// CORE CAPABILITIES

Offensive AI, Threat Emulation & Hardware Research

We benchmark offensive AI models, virtualize edge NPU silicon, emulate advanced threat group behaviors, and build open-source toolkits to help organizations validate their security posture and train defense teams.

🤖

Offensive AI & Agentic Security Research

AI and LLM-integrated applications introduce high-risk vulnerabilities at 2.7x the rate of traditional software assets. Our lab benchmarks local Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) vs. dense reasoning models (Strata Benchmarks), tests autonomous multi-agent offensive swarms (Reasoning vs. Frontier Models), and engineers deterministic API-layer kill switches and scope guardrails to contain rogue agentic workflows.

🧠

Edge NPU Silicon & Hardware Reverse Engineering

We push offensive and defensive AI to the bare-metal silicon edge. Our research spans virtualizing 24 TOPS PCIe NPUs (such as the Axera AX8850) inside Proxmox VE LXC containers with zero-copy CMM memory pipelines, auditing vendor C/C++ NPU runtime SDKs for DMA and memory corruption vulnerabilities (Hunting Edge AI CVEs & Dissecting the AX-LLM CVEs on Cobalt), and reverse-engineering abandoned IoT hardware ecosystems like the JoeBro Sobro Smart Table PWA.

⚔️

Threat Emulation Research

Adversary emulation is the cornerstone of proactive cyber defense. We specialize in researching, modeling, and recreating advanced persistent threat (APT) campaigns and authentication attack flows. By analyzing real-world tactics, techniques, and procedures (TTPs) at the byte level, we provide organizations with objective playbooks and cross-platform Windows (Event ID 4625) & Linux SSH telemetry emulators to validate detection pipelines.

🛡️

Purple Teaming & Collaborative Defense

True resilience is achieved when offensive insight meets defensive engineering. We develop structured purple teaming methodologies and collaborative feedback loops designed to break down security silos. Our research focuses on building open-source validation playbooks, mapping control coverage to the MITRE ATT&CK framework, and establishing continuous telemetry validation protocols that empower blue teams to isolate coverage gaps before adversaries exploit them.

🔬

Private AI Labs & Open-Source Tooling

Contributing back to the security community is vital for collective defense. We design zero-telemetry, air-gapped offensive research environments (SearXNG & LiteLLM Gateways on NextGenPVE) and release open-source security utilities, multi-language threat emulators, and hardware rescue tools on GitHub.

♿

Accessible Security & 500-WPM Screen-Reader Engineering

Led by Joe Brinkley (The Blind Hacker), we pioneer high-velocity, screen-reader-first (Hacking by Ear at 500 WPM) Linux CLI methodologies and AI-compiled accessible CLIs & PWAs for blind and visually impaired security engineers. Explore our dedicated Hacking While Blind CLI Survival Guide to see how pure text streams and terminal automation outperform visual GUIs.
