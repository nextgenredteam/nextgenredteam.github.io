## Description

At Cobalt.io, as a Senior Security Researcher, you will be conducting advanced vulnerability research and security assessments across modern application and operating system stacks, cloud infrastructure, and critical systems.

Your role focuses on identifying impactful security flaws, developing potential exploit techniques, and collaborating with cross-functional teams to strengthen customer security postures. Operating at the tip of the spear within Cobalt's Intelligence Engine, you will bridge the gap between cutting-edge adversary tradecraft, automated continuous penetration testing (CPT), and practical remediation guidance.

---

## What You'll Do

- **Advanced Vulnerability Research:** Conduct deep-dive vulnerability research, reverse engineering, and threat analysis across modern Web/API platforms, mobile operating systems, low-level OS stacks, cloud infrastructures (GCP/AWS/Azure/K8s), and critical enterprise software systems.
- **Exploit Crafting & Validation:** Identify high-impact, zero-day, and novel attack surfaces; develop proof-of-concept (PoC) exploit techniques to demonstrate real-world risk without causing production disruption.
- **Continuous Pentesting (CPT) Innovation:** Collaborate with Product and Engineering to translate manual research findings into programmatically verifiable test cases ("Automation Hooks") and feed fuzzer/scanner automation engines.
- **Methodology Refresh & Tradecraft Leadership:** Maintain and refresh Cobalt's testing methodologies, including AI/LLM risk frameworks, Model Context Protocol (MCP) integrations, agentic workflow security, cloud security baselines, and advanced API security guidelines.
- **Cross-Functional Collaboration:** Partner with Product, Engineering, Delivery Operations, and Technical Program Managers (TPMs) to integrate research intelligence directly into the Cobalt platform.
- **Community Enablement & Mentorship:** Provide guidance, technical benchmarking, and mentorship to Associate Security Researchers and Cobalt Core community leads; participate in quality assurance (QA) reviews for complex engagements.
- **Thought Leadership & Public Outreach:** Represent Cobalt in the cybersecurity research community through high-impact technical blog posts, whitepapers, threat advisories, and conference presentations (e.g., DEF CON, Black Hat, BSides).

## You Have

- **Experience:** 6+ years of dedicated experience in offensive security, with a demonstrated research component that goes beyond standard penetration testing engagements. Candidates whose background is primarily delivery-focused pentesting without published research output, CVE disclosures, or open-source security tooling contributions will not meet this bar. Candidates with a minimum of 5 years of total experience may be considered only with a strong, verifiable research track record (published CVEs, public tooling, or conference-level presentations).
- **Technical Depth across Modern Stacks:** Demonstrated expertise in modern application stacks (Node.js, Go, Python, Java, Rust), operating system security fundamentals (Linux/Windows/macOS internals), and containerized cloud environments (Docker, Kubernetes, AWS/GCP).
- **Exploit Crafting & Analysis:** Proven ability to analyze binary, source code, or bytecode to construct reliable PoC exploits for complex vulnerability classes (e.g., memory corruption, deserialization, auth bypass, SSRF/RCE, cloud privilege escalation).
- **Tooling & Automation Skills:** Strong proficiency in Python, Go, Bash, or Rust for building automation scripts, custom fuzzers, and research tooling.
- **Clear Technical Communication:** Ability to document complex technical findings into actionable remediation guidance for engineers, product teams, and executive stakeholders.
- **US-Based:** Strictly limited to candidates residing in the United States (EST or CST time zone alignment preferred for morning team collaboration).
